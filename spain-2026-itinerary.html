Notes — Aug 29

Days 4–14 – Aug 30 to Sep 9 (Camino Francés – Roncesvalles to Burgos)

Warning

Tentative pacing. Lodging is only confirmed through Roncesvalles (Aug 29). The stages below are a suggested ~20–25 km/day plan with no rest days, ending in Burgos on Day 12 of walking. Late August on the Camino Francés is peak season and hot, especially crossing the Meseta near Burgos — worth booking a day or two ahead rather than showing up unbooked, and starting early to beat the heat. Adjust freely once on the trail.

  • Day 4 (Aug 30): Roncesvalles → Zubiri (~21.5 km / 13.4 mi)
  • Day 5 (Aug 31): Zubiri → Pamplona (~20.4 km / 12.7 mi)
  • Day 6 (Sep 1): Pamplona → Puente la Reina (~23.8 km / 14.8 mi)
  • Day 7 (Sep 2): Puente la Reina → Estella (~21.5 km / 13.4 mi)
  • Day 8 (Sep 3): Estella → Los Arcos (~21.3 km / 13.2 mi)
  • Day 9 (Sep 4): Los Arcos → Logroño (~27.5 km / 17.1 mi)
  • Day 10 (Sep 5): Logroño → Nájera (~29 km / 18.0 mi)
  • Day 11 (Sep 6): Nájera → Santo Domingo de la Calzada (~21 km / 13.0 mi)
  • Day 12 (Sep 7): Santo Domingo de la Calzada → Belorado (~22.8 km / 14.2 mi)
  • Day 13 (Sep 8): Belorado → San Juan de Ortega (~23.7 km / 14.7 mi)
  • Day 14 (Sep 9): San Juan de Ortega → Burgos (~25.8 km / 16.0 mi)
Note

Notes — Sep 11

Packing List

Documents & Money

  • Passport
  • Pilgrim credential (credencial) — get stamped daily
  • Pilgrim Pass QR code (register before departure, see note above)
  • Flight, train & lodging confirmations (digital copies on phone)
  • Credit + ATM cards
  • Euro cash (small bills for albergues, donativos)

Pack Setup

  • Backpack with rain cover (35–40L)
  • 1 small dry sack (electronics/passport)
  • Packing cubes + plastic bags (organization, laundry separation)

Clothing

  • 2 quick-dry short-sleeve tees
  • 1 long-sleeve thermal / light layer (cool mornings crossing the Pyrenees on Day 1)
  • 1 light fleece or packable insulated layer
  • 1 lightweight rain jacket
  • 1 convertible hiking pant
  • 1 pair town/casual pants or shorts
  • 3 pairs quick-dry underwear
  • 3 pairs hiking socks (merino blend)

Footwear

  • Trail runners or light hiking shoes (broken in well before the trip)
  • Lightweight sandals or flip-flops (evenings)

Toiletries

  • Toothbrush + travel toothpaste
  • Small liquid soap (body + laundry, e.g. Dr. Bronner’s)
  • Travel deodorant
  • Quick-dry towel
  • Sunscreen (August sun is strong, especially crossing the Meseta near Burgos)
  • Lip balm with SPF

Health & First Aid

  • Blister pads (Compeed-style hydrocolloid) — essential for Day 1’s Pyrenees crossing
  • Ibuprofen / acetaminophen / personal medications
  • Hand sanitizer, tissues
  • Earplugs + eye mask (shared albergue dorms)
  • Trekking poles (recommended for the SJPP–Roncesvalles descent)

Electronics

  • iPhone + USB-C cord
  • EU plug adapter
  • AirPods
  • Portable power bank

Other

  • Sunglasses
  • Sun hat
  • Sleep sack / liner (albergue bedding varies)
  • 2 × 1L water bottles (heat + climb on Day 1 demand real hydration capacity)
  • Pen (credencial stamps, customs form on the flight home)
